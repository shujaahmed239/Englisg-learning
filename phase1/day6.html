Im gonna + verb


im gonna ka matlab hota future ma(aane wala kaam) ke liye use hota hai
Yani: Main abhi / jaldi / future mein koi kaam karne wala hoon.


Example:


  • I'm gonna eat.
  • I'm gonna call you.
  • I'm gonna study.
  • I'm gonna sleep.
  • I'm gonna go home.
