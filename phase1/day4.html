Im trying + verb


Im trying + verb ka matlab hota ha kisi kam ko karna chahna / kisi cheez ko try karna yh
jab use karta ha kisi kam ko karna ke koshish kar raha hoty han.


Example:


  • I'm trying to learn english.
  • I'm trying but its hard.
  • I'm trying to swim.
  • I'm trying to drive.
  • I'm trying to ride a bike.
