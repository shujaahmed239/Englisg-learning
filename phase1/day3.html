Im Getting


Is ka matlab hota ha muja ho raha ha, muja mil raha hai, main ho raha hun "Hasil karna resive karna"


Example:


  • I'm getting better
  • I'm getting a money
  • I'm getting late
  • I'm getting a gift
  • I'm getting cold
