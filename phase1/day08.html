I have + noun


“I have + (noun)” ka matlab “I have” = “Mere paas hai” ya “Mujhe hai” (situation ke hisaab se)
Yani jab aap kisi cheez ka hona, milna, ya possess karna batate ho.



Example:


  • I have a headache.
  • I have a meeting.
  • I have a car.
  • I have a book.
  • I have no time.
