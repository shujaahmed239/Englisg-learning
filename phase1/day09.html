I have + (Past Participle)


I have + (Past Participle) ka matlab Ye structure Present Perfect Tense hota hai. “I have + past participle” = “Main ne … kiya hai” Yani koi kaam ho chuka hai, lekin uska result abhi tak important hai.


Example:


  • I have finished my homework.
  • I have eaten lunch.
  • I have read a book.
  • I have seen that movie.
  • I have visited lahore.
