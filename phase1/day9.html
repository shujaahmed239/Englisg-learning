I used to + verb


“I used to + verb" ka matlab “I used to” = “Main pehle karta tha” Yani koi kaam jo past mein regular hota tha, lekin ab nahi hota.p>

Example:


  • I used to play cricket.
  • I used to wake up early.
  • I used to live in Karachi.
  • I used to watch cartoons.
  • I used to go to school by bus.
