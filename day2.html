Good at


Good at ka matlab hota ha kisi kam mein, acha hona / maharat rakhna / kisi cheze ma mahir hona na


Example:


  • I'm good at playing pupg.
  • I'm good at coding.
  • I'm good at swiming.
  • I'm good at driwing.
  • I'm good at riding bike.
